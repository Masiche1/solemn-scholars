How do I know a tutor is qualified?

All tutors submit qualifications and ID for review. Verified tutors display a green badge on their profile.

How much does tutoring cost?

Tutors set their own rates, typically between $30 and $60 per hour. You'll see the rate before you book.

Can I have lessons online?

Yes. Most tutors offer online lessons, and many also teach in person. Filter by your preferred mode.
